Mental Health Information

Understanding mental health is the first step toward wellness. Learn about common conditions, symptoms, and effective strategies for maintaining mental wellbeing.

Common Mental Health Conditions

😰

Anxiety Disorders

Excessive worry, fear, or nervousness that interferes with daily activities. Includes generalized anxiety, panic attacks, and phobias.

Affects 1 in 5 adults Highly treatable
😔

Depression

Persistent sadness, loss of interest, and feelings of worthlessness that affect daily functioning and quality of life.

Affects 1 in 6 adults Most common mental disorder
😫

Stress Disorders

Emotional and physical strain caused by overwhelming pressures, including PTSD and acute stress responses.

77% experience stress Manageable with techniques
😴

Sleeping Disorders

Conditions affecting sleep quality, timing, and duration, including insomnia, sleep apnea, and narcolepsy.

Affects 50-70 million adults Impacts overall health
🍽️

Eating Disorders

Severe disturbances in eating behaviors, including anorexia, bulimia, and binge eating disorders.

Affects 9% of population Serious but treatable
🔥

Burnout

Emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by excessive and prolonged stress, particularly in work environments.

Affects 76% of workers Preventable with balance
🔄

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

Pattern of unwanted thoughts and fears (obsessions) that lead to repetitive behaviors (compulsions).

Affects 2.3% of population Treatable with therapy
💊

Substance Use Disorders

Problematic use of alcohol, drugs, or other substances that leads to significant impairment or distress.

Affects 20 million Americans Recovery is possible

Mental Health Wellness Tips

🏃‍♀️

Regular Exercise

Physical activity releases endorphins and reduces stress hormones. Aim for 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily.

😴

Quality Sleep

Maintain 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Establish a consistent sleep schedule and create a relaxing bedtime routine.

🥗

Balanced Nutrition

Eat a variety of nutritious foods. Omega-3 fatty acids, vitamins, and minerals support brain health and mood regulation.

🧘‍♀️

Mindfulness & Meditation

Practice mindfulness to reduce anxiety and improve emotional regulation. Even 10 minutes daily can make a difference.

👥

Social Connections

Maintain meaningful relationships. Social support is crucial for mental resilience and overall wellbeing.

📝

Journaling

Express thoughts and feelings through writing. Journaling helps process emotions and identify patterns in thinking.

When to Seek Professional Help

Warning Signs

  • Persistent sadness or hopelessness lasting more than two weeks
  • Significant changes in sleep or appetite
  • Loss of interest in activities once enjoyed
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • Withdrawal from friends and family
  • Unexplained physical symptoms like headaches or stomach issues
  • Thoughts of self-harm or suicide
  • Substance abuse or risky behaviors
