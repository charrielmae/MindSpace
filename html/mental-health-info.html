Mental Health Information

Understanding mental health is the first step toward wellness. Learn about common conditions, symptoms, and effective strategies for maintaining mental wellbeing.

Common Mental Health Conditions

😨

Anxiety Disorders

Mental disorders in which an individual has extreme, chronic anxiety, worry, or uneasiness that is either recurrent or more severe than usual.

Affects 1 in 5 adults Highly treatable
😩

Depression

An individual experiences intense sorrow, poor energy levels, and an absence in passion in activities they once found enjoyable.

Affects 1 in 6 adults Most common mental disorder
😖

Stress Disorders

Stressful or traumatic situations cause intense physical or mental emotions in people, making it challenging for them to feel safe or at ease even after a situation is concluded.

77% experience stress Manageable with techniques
💤

Sleeping Disorders

Conditions affecting sleep quality, timing, and duration, including insomnia, sleep apnea, and narcolepsy.

Affects 50-70 million adults Impacts overall health
🍴

Eating Disorders

Mental health disorders when a person's eating habits, body image, or dysfunctional relationship with food can have an impact on their physical and emotional well-being. It may include overeating excessively or insufficiently or feeling severe fear over one's physique and visual appeal.

Affects 9% of population Serious but treatable
😮‍💨

Burnout

A condition of physical, mental, and emotional fatigue induced on by constant stress, commonly from obligations, work, or school. A person may feel drained of energy demotivated, stressed out, and less likely to concentrate or carry out everyday tasks, as a result.

Affects 76% of workers Preventable with balance
🫨

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

Is a mental illness in which a person has undesirable, frequent thoughts (called obsessions) and feels obliged to engage in particular behaviors (called compulsions) in order to mitigate psychological distress.

Affects 2.3% of population Treatable with therapy
💊

Substance Use Disorders

Is a mental illness in which a person struggles to regulate their use of drugs, alcohol, or other illegal substances, even when doing so negatively influences their everyday life, relationships, or health. Dependency, impulses, and trouble quitting on their own are possible consequences.

Affects 20 million Americans Recovery is possible

Mental Health Wellness Tips

🚴🏽‍♂️

Regular Exercise

Physical activity releases endorphins and reduces stress hormones. Aim for 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily.

😴

Quality Sleep

Maintain 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Establish a consistent sleep schedule and create a relaxing bedtime routine.

🥪

Balanced Nutrition

Obtaining and utilizing food nutrients (such as minerals, vitamins, proteins, lipids, and dietary fiber) to maintain the body's strength, health, and proper functionality.

🧘🏽‍♂️

Mindfulness & Meditation

Practice mindfulness to reduce anxiety and improve emotional regulation. Even 10 minutes daily can make a difference.

👯‍♂️

Social Connections

Maintain meaningful relationships. Social support is crucial for mental resilience and overall wellbeing.

🗒️

Journaling

Express thoughts and feelings through writing. Journaling helps makes you feel lighter mentally and improves your understanding of oneself.

When to Seek Professional Help

Warning Signs

  • Persistent sadness or hopelessness lasting more than two weeks.
  • Significant changes in sleep or appetite.
  • Physical symptoms like headaches or body tension without clear cause.
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions.
  • Withdrawal from friends and family.
  • Repetitive actions (hand washing, checking, counting).
  • Thoughts of self-harm or suicide.
  • Substance abuse or risky behaviors.
